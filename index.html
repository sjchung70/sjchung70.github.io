About

Music between
memory and change.

CHUNG Seung Jae is a Korean composer whose music explores the meeting point of tradition and contemporary expression. Repetition and transformation form a central part of his musical language, allowing familiar materials to acquire new identities through time.

Drawing frequently on Korean local folk songs, he reinterprets inherited melodic and emotional traces with a contemporary sensibility, seeking a musical language that is both grounded and newly imagined. A full biography and artistic statement will be added here.
